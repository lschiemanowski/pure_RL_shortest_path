# Reinforcement-learning trainer

The reinforcement-learning trainer improves the transformer policy from
experience generated by the policy itself. It requests rollouts on training
problems, obtains declared graph-derived feedback for the sampled completions,
and uses a reinforcement-learning objective to update the policy. Demonstrated
solutions, teacher reasoning, and supervised target completions do not enter
this process.

The trainer owns the composition of verifier reward, reward shaping, and
optional auxiliary graph-derived objectives, keeping every learning signal
separately named and configurable. It also governs training progression,
including the selection and advancement of curriculum stages, while preserving
the distinction between training experience and independent evaluation
evidence.
